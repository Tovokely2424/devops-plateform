# Lab 01 — Deployment Scaling

## Objective

Deploy an NGINX workload and manage its replica count.

## Tasks

- Create a Deployment
- Scale from 2 to 5 replicas
- Verify ReplicaSet behaviour
- Verify Pod distribution
- Scale back to 2 replicas

## Validation

Deployment reached the expected number of replicas.
